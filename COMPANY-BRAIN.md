# MimaarLink Company Brain

The one-page picture of the company. Read this before any business, strategy, outreach or product decision. Keep it short and current; details live in the linked files.

Last updated: 2026-10-07 · Owner: Jassim Abdulrahman Al-Anbari, Founder & CEO · CR 243332

## 1. What we do

MimaarLink connects project owners with contractors and consultants across Qatar's built environment. An owner describes a project; we turn it into a clear brief, send it to vetted providers, and the owner receives three to five comparable offers to choose from.

**Our core advantage is the market record.** Every request, invite, bid, choice and outcome is recorded in a structured way. Today this information is lost in WhatsApp chats and personal contacts; recorded, it makes the market clearer for everyone and is hard to copy.

We accept the full range of real project work (fit-out, MEP, commercial, villas and residential, clinics and F&B, industrial, mixed-use, general contracting, specialist trades, and architecture / engineering / design / supervision / approvals / tendering). The full list is in `AGENTS.md`.

## 2. The market we work in

Qatar's project sourcing is informal and fragmented: referrals, WhatsApp, personal contacts, unclear scopes, and providers who do a bit of everything. There are no clean categories yet.

Our job is to bring order: one clear starting point, structured briefs, qualified providers, comparable offers.

**Flexibility in what we accept; clarity in how we present it.** We will meet messy cases we have not planned for. Handle them case by case, write down what happened, and turn repeated patterns into rules once we have real market experience.

**Related platform:** QDB's Binaa connects housing-loan citizens with registered contractors and consultants for villa builds (only Binaa-registered firms can build housing-loan homes since June 2025). It is a directory for one segment; MimaarLink covers the rest of private work with comparable offers. Treat it as complementary. Details in `docs/strategy/binaa.md`.

## 3. Who we serve

- **Project owners:** villa owners, landlords, businesses opening or moving premises (offices, shops, clinics, cafés), developers. Posting a project is free.
- **Providers:** contractors and consultant offices with a Qatar CR. Applying is free.
- **Referral partners:** property managers, brokers, business centres, architects, showrooms. They see owners before the scope is ready.

## 4. How we make money

**Currently free for everyone.** No fee is charged to owners or providers while we collect real data.

The July plans assumed a QAR 750 fee paid by the selected provider before handoff. That is a **hypothesis only**. Decide pricing after real projects show who gets value and how much. Until then:

- Do not quote any price to anyone.
- Never hold or handle client project money.
- Record the data that will inform pricing later (see `docs/strategy/10-year-moat.md`, "Minimum data to capture now").

## 6. Current focus

1. **Get the first real projects.** Demand is the scarce side. Supply (3 providers) is enough to start matching manually. First channel: owners who already posted a need on public classifieds (`docs/playbooks/classifieds-owner-outreach.md`).
2. **Run each project by hand** using `docs/operations.md`, and log what happens.
3. **Grow providers in step with projects**, not ahead of them.
4. **Do not overbuild.** No big new software until real projects show what is needed.

Marketing may push a few clear entry points (for example villa renovation, office/shop fit-out, "compare before you choose") while intake stays open to everything.

## 7. Long-term direction

Become Qatar's trusted layer for private-project sourcing, then expand one GCC country at a time through a local partner. The advantage that is hard to copy is the record of real projects: how providers respond, how bids compare, who gets chosen and what happened next.

Growth gates (adapted from the strategy file; "paid" becomes "successful" while we are free):

1. First successful introduction (owner chooses a provider and they connect).
2. 10 introductions: stable categories, scoring rules, bid template.
3. 50 introductions in two repeatable categories: build provider dashboard and structured bidding.
4. 100 introductions with repeat demand: test pricing and subscriptions.
5. Qatar density, then one GCC country.

## 8. Firm rules

- Jassim alone decides on spending money, contacting people, publishing ads or posts, pricing, legal wording, and sharing private data.
- No guarantees: not of projects for providers, nor of quality, lowest price or completion for owners. MimaarLink connects; the agreement is between owner and provider.
- Never invent statistics, testimonials, client logos or counts.
- Collect only the data needed; keep owner details private until the owner chooses a provider.
- Never hold or process project funds.
- Do not change the brand outside `brand/BRAND.md`.
