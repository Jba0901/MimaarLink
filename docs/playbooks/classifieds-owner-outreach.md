# Owner Outreach from Public Classifieds

How Jassim finds the first real projects by contacting people who have already posted a project need in public (Mzad Qatar first; the same steps apply to Qatar Living classifieds and public groups). Decided 2026-10-04 (see `DECISIONS.md`).

The idea: the owner has already said in public that they need work done. We call, explain MimaarLink in one minute, and offer to do the coordination: we write the brief (or post it for them, with their consent), send it to suitable providers, and return three to five comparable offers. The owner chooses; the agreement is between owner and provider.

## 1. Rules

- Jassim makes the contact personally. No bulk messaging, no bots, no automated scraping of the site. Read and follow each site's terms of use.
- Contact only ads that are recent (posted in the last 14 days) and clearly ask for work to be done ("مطلوب مقاول", "مطلوب شركة صيانة", "ترميم فيلا"…).
- One call, then at most one WhatsApp follow-up. If they say no or do not answer, stop.
- Keep a do-not-contact list (number only) and check it before every call.
- Post on their behalf only after a clear yes to the consent question (section 4). Note the date and wording in the log.
- Never reuse their ad photos or text in our own marketing.
- Say plainly: the service is free, we do not guarantee price, quality or completion, and we never handle project money.
- Collect only what the brief needs. Qatar's personal data law (Law No. 13 of 2016) applies: purpose-limited, consent-based, deleted when no longer needed.
- **Housing-loan villas:** since June 2025, only contractors and consultants registered on QDB's Binaa platform can build homes financed by the QDB housing loan. If the owner mentions the housing loan, invite only Binaa-registered providers, or point them to Binaa.

## 2. Which ads to pick

| Good fit | Skip |
|---|---|
| Owner looking for a contractor, fit-out, renovation, extension, MEP, maintenance contract, design or supervision | Ads selling materials, equipment or used items |
| Clear location in Qatar | Labour supply or visa-related ads |
| A business opening premises (café, clinic, shop, office) | Ads older than 14 days or already marked as done |
| | Anything that looks fake or unclear |

**Contractors advertising their services** are provider leads, not owners. Send them the provider application link and the first message in `docs/operations.md` section 2.

## 3. Call scripts

Keep the call under 4 minutes. Three openers; use whichever feels natural, and note which one you used so we learn which works.

### Option A: direct

```text
السلام عليكم، معك جاسم من معمار لينك. شفت إعلانك في مزاد عن [ترميم الفيلا / تجهيز المحل]. 
نحن منصة قطرية: نكتب تفاصيل مشروعك بشكل واضح، نرسله لمقاولين مناسبين، وتوصلك من 3 إلى 5 عروض تقدر تقارن بينها. الخدمة مجانية، والاختيار والاتفاق يكون بينك وبين المقاول مباشرة.
عندك دقيقتين أسألك عن المشروع؟
```

```text
Hello, this is Jassim from MimaarLink. I saw your ad on Mzad about [the villa renovation / the shop fit-out].
We are a Qatari platform: we write your project up clearly, send it to suitable contractors, and you receive three to five offers you can compare. It is free, and you choose and agree directly with the contractor.
Do you have two minutes for a few questions about the project?
```

### Option B: ask first

```text
السلام عليكم، معك جاسم. شفت إعلانك عن [المشروع]. لقيت أحد ولا للحين تدور؟
[إذا للحين يدور] نحن في معمار لينك نجمع لك من 3 إلى 5 عروض من مقاولين مناسبين بدل ما تتصل بكل واحد بنفسك. مجاناً. تحب أشرح لك بسرعة؟
```

```text
Hello, this is Jassim. I saw your ad about [the project]. Have you found someone, or are you still looking?
[If still looking] At MimaarLink we collect three to five offers from suitable contractors for you, so you do not have to call each one yourself. It is free. Shall I explain quickly?
```

### Option C: WhatsApp first (when calling feels too cold)

```text
السلام عليكم، معك جاسم من معمار لينك (mimaarlink.com). شفت إعلانك في مزاد عن [المشروع].
نقدر نرسل تفاصيل مشروعك لمقاولين مناسبين في قطر، وتوصلك من 3 إلى 5 عروض تقارن بينها. الخدمة مجانية والاختيار لك.
إذا يناسبك، أتصل عليك دقيقتين وقت ما تحب.
```

```text
Hello, this is Jassim from MimaarLink (mimaarlink.com). I saw your ad on Mzad about [the project].
We can send your project details to suitable contractors in Qatar, and you receive three to five offers to compare. It is free and the choice is yours.
If it suits you, I can call for two minutes at a time that works for you.
```

## 4. Questions on the call, and consent

1. What exactly needs doing? (rooms, area, current state)
2. Where in Qatar?
3. When do you want to start?
4. Rough budget range, if you have one?
5. Photos or drawings you can send on WhatsApp?
6. Are you the decision maker? (owner, tenant with landlord approval, manager)
7. Housing loan involved? (see the Binaa rule above)

**Consent question (must be asked before posting on their behalf):**

```text
تسمح لي أكتب طلب مشروعك على معمار لينك بالنيابة عنك، وأرسل وصف المشروع بدون اسمك ورقمك لمقاولين مناسبين؟ بياناتك ما تنعطى لأي مقاول إلا بعد ما تختاره أنت.
```

```text
May I post your project request on MimaarLink on your behalf, and send the description to suitable contractors without your name or number? Your details go to a contractor only after you choose them.
```

If yes: submit the request at mimaarlink.com/post-project with their details and add "Posted on behalf: consent [date], call" in the notes. If they prefer to do it themselves, send the link.

## 5. After the call (WhatsApp, same day)

```text
شكراً على وقتك. هذا ملخص طلبك: [ملخص قصير]. 
بنرسله الحين لمقاولين مناسبين، وأرجع لك بالعروض. إذا عندك صور إضافية أرسلها هنا.
```

```text
Thank you for your time. Here is a summary of your request: [short summary].
We are sending it to suitable contractors now and will come back to you with the offers. If you have more photos, send them here.
```

Then run the project as in `docs/operations.md` sections 3 to 6.

## 6. What to log (every contact, even a no)

This record is the data advantage: it shows what the market actually asks for and how people respond.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date contacted | 2026-10-05 |
| Source and ad date | Mzad, posted 2026-10-02 |
| Category and zone | Villa renovation, Al Wakrah |
| Opener used | A / B / C |
| Result | Not reached · Not interested · Already found someone · Brief taken · Posted on behalf |
| Reason, if no | Their words, short |
| Consent | Yes / no, date |
| Linked project ID | From admin, if posted |

Log the source manually. The site's automatic source tracking only works when a visitor accepts cookies, so it cannot be relied on for these leads.
