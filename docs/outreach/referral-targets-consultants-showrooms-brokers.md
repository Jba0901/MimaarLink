## Week 1 Order of Attack

No outreach is included in this research task. If activated later, the recommended sequence is:

1. Start with targets 1-8: strongest direct owner touchpoints and specific public routes.
2. Use targets 9-12 for property-turnover and showroom-partnership tests.

## Guardrails for Any Later Outreach

- Say "referral and bid-comparison support," not "lead-selling marketplace."
- Promise no poaching: the referring organization retains its client relationship and technical/design role.
- Accept only mid-ticket, reachable-owner projects with a real location, timeline, and sufficient scope.
- Do not promise cheapest price, contractor quality, award, project outcome, or exclusivity.
- Track source organization, project category, scope status, owner consent, and referral outcome.
