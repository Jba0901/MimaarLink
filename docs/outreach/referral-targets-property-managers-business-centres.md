# MimaarLink Week 1 Owner / Referral Targets

Research date: 2026-07-11  
Scope: Qatar public-web research only. No outreach was performed.

## Selection Standard

- Prioritize private operators with direct visibility into landlord maintenance, villa upgrades, tenant turnover, office moves, retail leasing, or workspace growth.
- Favor public WhatsApp, contact-form, or business-email routes that can plausibly reach a local operator.
- Exclude government entities, major procurement-led enterprises, generic directories, and pure listing portals.
- Confidence: `A` means an official source directly confirms both the relevant operating role and a usable public route; `B` means the fit is credible but the referral path or public route is less direct; `C` means evidence is limited and the target needs re-validation before use.

## Week 1 Priority Order

Start with `1-6` because their official services put them closest to owner-approved maintenance, renovation, retail, and office upgrade decisions. Then test the workspace graduation trigger with `11`, `12`, `13`, and `15`. Keep `7-10` and `14` as the second wave: they are credible, but likely need a sharper explanation of how MimaarLink complements rather than competes with their existing property or facilities capability.

## Guardrail for Any Later Outreach

The partnership ask should be a narrow referral path, not free project management: MimaarLink helps a real owner or tenant define enough scope to receive comparable contractor or consultant responses. It does not guarantee contractor quality, lowest price, project delivery, or payment outcomes.
