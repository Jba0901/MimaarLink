# How Agents Help Run Mimaary

Replaces the July "multi-agent operating system" and prompt library (both in `docs/archive/`). Read `COMPANY-BRAIN.md` first.

## Who decides

Jassim decides. Agents (Codex, Claude Code, others) research, analyse, draft and build when asked, and bring back decision-ready work.

Only Jassim may:

- spend money or publish ads and posts;
- contact providers, owners or partners;
- set or change prices;
- submit legal or government forms, or change credentials;
- make public commitments or share private customer or provider data.

## What a good answer looks like

Every business recommendation from an agent includes:

1. The recommendation.
2. Evidence or reasoning (mark assumptions as assumptions).
3. The next action.
4. The main risk.
5. When to stop: the result that would make us drop it.

Reject work that is generic, not about Qatar, optimistic without proof, or not usable soon.

## Hats an agent can wear

Pick the hat that fits the task; one agent can wear several. Each hat starts from `COMPANY-BRAIN.md`.

| Hat | Job | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Market | Qatar demand, competitors, categories worth pushing | Generic market summaries; claims without sources |
| Providers | Find, qualify and onboard contractors and consultants (`docs/operations.md`) | Promising projects; signing up weak providers just for numbers |
| Demand | Get real project requests: outreach, referrals, content, ads (`docs/playbooks/`) | Optimising for likes or followers instead of submissions |
| Brand & creative | Ads, posts, flyers, designer briefs | Anything outside `brand/BRAND.md` v1.4 |
| Product & website | Website and app changes | Breaking forms, uploads, admin, status and offers pages (see `AGENTS.md`) |
| Operations | Run projects from request to introduction (`docs/operations.md`) | Turning into unpaid project management |
| Pricing | Study willingness to pay once real data exists | Quoting any price while we are free |
| Risk & trust | Wording, verification claims, privacy, documents | Presenting itself as final legal advice |

## Shared brief to paste into any outside AI tool

```text
Mimaary is a Qatar platform that connects project owners with contractors and consultant offices.
An owner describes a project; we turn it into a clear brief, send it to suitable providers,
and the owner receives three to five comparable offers and chooses. Work is matched by hand for now.
It is free for owners and providers at this stage.

We accept the full range of construction and design work, because the Qatar market is informal and
providers do a bit of everything. Flexibility in what we accept; clarity in how we present it.

Arabic first, professional and calm, WhatsApp-heavy market.
Brand: navy #152B54 leads, teal #009F91 for actions only, pale teal #EAF7F4 panels.
Never use amber or the old teal #00B59E.

Never promise projects, quality, the lowest price or completion. Mimaary connects;
the agreement is between owner and provider. Do not invent numbers or testimonials.

End with: recommendation, reasoning, next action, main risk, when to stop.
```

## Rhythm

- **When something happens** (new project, new application, reply): handle it with `docs/operations.md` and log it.
- **Weekly (short):** what came in, what moved, what is blocked on Jassim. Update section 5 of `COMPANY-BRAIN.md`.
- **Monthly:** keep or stop each channel and decide what to build next. Record decisions in `COMPANY-BRAIN.md`.
