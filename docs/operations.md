# Running a Project by Hand

How MimaarLink handles a project from request to introduction while matching is manual. Drawn from the July first-wave plan (`docs/archive/`) and updated for the free model and open intake.

Target: under 2 hours of work per project.

## 1. Provider qualification

**Before scoring, collect:** company name, Qatar phone and contact person, CR number, main services, recent Qatar work. For consultants, also discipline and registration or classification where relevant.

**Score out of 100:**

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Service fit | 20 |
| Evidence of Qatar work | 20 |
| Responsiveness | 15 |
| Quality process | 15 |
| Capacity | 10 |
| Commercial discipline (clear quotes, terms) | 10 |
| Trust signals | 10 |

- **A (80–100):** priority.
- **B (65–79):** usable backup.
- **C (50–64):** keep, do not match yet.
- **Below 50 or a serious red flag:** reject.

Providers often do many things. Record every service they credibly do, not only one category.

## 2. Provider outreach

**First message (Arabic):**

```text
السلام عليكم، معك جاسم من MimaarLink.

نحن نبني قائمة مزودين في قطر لمطابقة أصحاب المشاريع مع مقاولين ومكاتب استشارية مناسبة حسب نوع المشروع والموقع والميزانية.

لا نعد بمشاريع، لكن نرغب بتقييم شركتكم للفرص المناسبة مستقبلاً. هل أقدر أسألكم 5 أسئلة سريعة عن خدماتكم، نطاق العمل، وأمثلة أعمالكم؟
```

**First message (English):**

```text
Hi, this is Jassim from MimaarLink.

We are building a Qatar provider shortlist to match project owners with suitable contractors and consultant offices based on scope, location and budget.

We are not promising projects, but we would like to check whether your company fits future opportunities. Can I ask 5 quick questions about your services, project size, availability and past work?
```

**Call questions** (keep it under 8 minutes):

1. Exact company name and main services?
2. Which work do you want more of right now?
3. Which areas do you cover?
4. Ideal project size? Too small or too large?
5. How fast can you visit a site, and quote after the visit?
6. Recent Qatar photos, BOQs, completion letters or references?
7. Own team, subcontractors or both?
8. Who prepares and approves quotes?
9. Usual warranty or defects period?
10. Consultants: which disciplines are you registered for, and do you handle approvals?
11. Who should receive our requests on WhatsApp?

**Pricing research (optional, only with Jassim's approval):** we are free now. If testing willingness to pay, ask how they value a qualified opportunity. Never quote a price or imply a fee is coming. Record the answer as yes, depends on size, only if free, or not interested.

## 3. Project intake

1. Open new requests quickly (aim for 15 minutes in working hours).
2. Confirm the owner is reachable.
3. Confirm what, where, photos or drawings, timing and budget range. Location defaults to Doha if skipped.
4. **Unusual or mixed requests:** accept them. Split them into clear work packages (for example "fit-out + MEP + approvals"), and write down how you handled the case.
5. Ask permission to share an anonymised brief with providers.
6. Set readiness:
   - **Bid ready:** clear scope and drawings.
   - **Quote ready:** clear enough for a site-visit quote.
   - **Scoping needed:** needs a consultant or more information first.
   - **Hold:** risk or not real.
7. Write the anonymised brief. Move to matching only when the owner and scope are real.

## 4. Matching

- **Filters:** services, location, project size, readiness.
- **Shortlist:** 5–8 providers at most, aiming for three to five offers.
- **Provider statuses per project:** Candidate · Brief sent · Interested · Bid requested · Bid received · Clarification needed · Shortlisted · Chosen · Introduced · Not chosen · No response.

## 5. Collecting bids

**Each quote should state:** price, what is included, exclusions, duration, payment terms, materials and labour assumptions, whether a site visit is needed, and how long the quote is valid.

**Limits:** one clarification round and at most two follow-ups per provider.

Enter bids in admin so they appear on the owner's offers page.

## 6. Comparison and handoff

- **The offers page shows each offer side by side:** price, duration, inclusions, exclusions, terms and notes. Sorting is neutral; there is no "best" badge.
- **Owner wording:**

  ```text
  بناءً على المعلومات المقدمة، هذه الخيارات تبدو الأقرب لنطاق مشروعك. الاختيار النهائي والاتفاق النهائي يكون بينك وبين مزود الخدمة مباشرة.
  ```

- **When the owner shortlists a firm** (stored on the server), confirm by WhatsApp, then introduce owner and provider directly.
- **Share owner contact details** and the exact location only after the owner has chosen.

## 7. Time budget per project

| Step | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Screen | 10 |
| Owner intake | 15 |
| Brief | 15 |
| Shortlist and send | 25 |
| Chase bids | 20 |
| Comparison | 25 |
| Introduction | 10 |
| Notes | 10 |

**Hard limits:**

- At most 8 providers per project.
- No free consulting or design work before the project is qualified.

## 8. When to pause

**Pause a project if:**

- the owner is unreachable after 3 attempts over 48 hours;
- the scope stays vague after one request for information;
- the budget is clearly unrealistic and the owner will not adjust;
- fewer than 2 relevant providers respond after widening the shortlist once;
- anyone asks MimaarLink to hold money, guarantee results or manage the contract.

**Review a channel or category if:**

- fewer than 3 of 30 targeted providers qualify;
- fewer than 3 interested replies come from 30 approaches;
- handling regularly goes over 2 hours.

## 9. What to log for every project

Write this down even while matching is manual; it is the data the long-term plan depends on (see `docs/strategy/10-year-moat.md`).

- **Source:** where the project came from.
- **Request:** category and work packages, zone, size and budget range, readiness.
- **Each provider:** when invited, when they responded, bid or no bid and why, how complete the quote was.
- **Outcome:** shortlisted and chosen provider, whether the project was awarded, and any later problems.
- **Effort:** handling time, plus anything unusual and how you solved it.
