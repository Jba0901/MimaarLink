# Snoonu: Possible Partner and Investor

Research note, 2026-10-10. Jassim raised Snoonu as a possible partner (a route to owners) and funder. Nothing is agreed, applied for or sent. Jassim alone decides on any contact. Check [snoonu.com](https://snoonu.com) and [startups.snoonu.com](https://startups.snoonu.com) again before quoting anything externally.

## In one minute

- Snoonu is a Qatari super app (founded 2019 by Hamad Mubarak Al-Hajri) that calls itself the number one delivery platform in Qatar. Since October 2025 it is **76.56% owned by Jahez**, a Saudi-listed company; the founder holds the rest.
- It already sells services, not only delivery: S Laundry, Car Services, and **House Services** (marked "New" in the Qatar app). In Kuwait (launched 30 April 2026), on-demand home services (electricians, plumbers, maintenance) were one of the four launch services.
- Those are quick, on-demand jobs. Mimaary covers what that model does not: **bigger jobs that need a brief and comparable quotes** (renovation, fit-out, MEP, design, supervision). Complementary, not competing.
- **The funding route is the Snoonu Startup Factory** (April 2026): up to USD 50K (early stage) or 100K (growth stage) as a convertible loan; "vertical marketplaces" is a named priority. Catches: founders must work **full-time, in person at Snoonu HQ**; a technical co-founder is strongly preferred; distribution inside the Snoonu app is offered only in the growth (post-revenue) track.
- Recommendation: check House Services in the app first, keep getting the first projects, then make one approach: apply to the Startup Factory if the conditions suit you, otherwise propose a small referral pilot. Do not ask for money in the first message.

## Where Mimaary fits

Snoonu's services model is on-demand: book a visit, a fixed job, done in hours. Project work does not fit that: it needs a scope, often a site visit, several quotes, and days or weeks to decide.

| | Snoonu House Services (check in the app) | Mimaary |
|---|---|---|
| Typical job | A visit: plumber, electrician, maintenance | A project: renovation, extension, fit-out, MEP, design, supervision |
| Price | Booked in the app (assumption: fixed or per visit) | Three to five comparable offers on one written brief |
| Time to decide | Hours | Days to weeks |
| Who agrees | Customer books and pays Snoonu | Owner chooses and agrees directly with the provider |

Three places where Snoonu meets project demand:

1. **House Services users whose job is bigger than a visit** (redo the bathrooms, renovate the majlis, extend the villa).
2. **Snoonu's merchants:** restaurants, cafés, shops and pharmacies opening or refitting branches. This is Mimaary's fit-out entry point, and Snoonu gains too, because every new branch means more orders.
3. **Snoonu itself:** dark stores (Snoomart), hubs, the new Snoonu Technology Park HQ, Kuwait and Oman rollouts. They probably have their own procurement; a long shot.

## Options, most realistic first

1. **Referral pilot (nothing to build on either side).** A "Bigger job? Get 3 to 5 offers" link from House Services, and a note to merchants opening a branch, both pointing to www.mimaary.com/post-project with a Snoonu tag. Free for users; 90 days; monthly referral counts shared. Snoonu gets an answer for jobs it cannot serve, a benefit for its merchants, and a local-startup story; we get a steady source of owners.
2. **Startup Factory (money and mentoring).** Early Stage track, details below. Getting in would also make the pilot much easier to agree.
3. **Deeper integration (later).** A Mimaary flow inside the Snoonu app, an API, or Snoonu Cloud. Not now: it costs build time on both sides and breaks our "do not overbuild" rule. Revisit at growth gate 3 (50 introductions).

Not a fit: Royal Club loyalty (we have nothing to discount while free); the merchant self-serve form (no services category).

## Startup Factory: details and fit

From [startups.snoonu.com](https://startups.snoonu.com), checked 2026-10-10:

- "Corporate venture and incubation programme backed by Snoonu", launched April 2026 and based at Snoonu Technology Park, Snoonu's new HQ.
- Priority sectors: eCommerce and marketplaces (including "vertical marketplaces"), AI solutions, social commerce.
- **Early Stage:** up to USD 50K; programme mentoring; legal, finance and compliance support.
- **Growth Stage (post-revenue):** up to USD 100K; distribution through the Snoonu app; go-to-market across the GCC and MENA; engineering, product, hiring and advisor support.
- **Money comes as a convertible loan**, not upfront equity: it turns into shares at the next funding round at a 15–20% discount or a valuation cap. Their Investment Committee sets the terms.
- Who can apply: a company registered in the GCC; tech-enabled; a team of 2+ with at least one full-time technical co-founder (solo founders "strongly" discouraged, not excluded); a working MVP or early users; a problem relevant across the region.
- **In person and full-time at Snoonu HQ in Doha** (their FAQ answer is "Yes").
- Free to apply, rolling intake, no deadline. Contact: business@snoonu.com, +974 4430 2000.
- Deals so far: Sufra AI (USD 100K pre-seed, restaurant smart menus). Hamad Al-Hajri's GrowthX also put USD 1M into Hired Valley and Talebhub and brought both into the Factory.

Decide your limit before you see any terms (same rule as `docs/strategy/funding-options-2026.md`). Taking the money also makes Snoonu, and through it Jahez, a future shareholder: good for distribution, and visible to any later investor such as QDB.

## Risks

- **They build it themselves.** Snoonu already runs House Services and leads with home services in Kuwait. The idea is not secret; what is hard to copy is our provider network and the record of real projects. Share the model, never provider lists, briefs or data, and arrive with real projects.
- **They pick someone else.** Syaanh (صيانة.كوم) is a Qatar app where maintenance companies already send priced offers on customer jobs (plumbing, electrical, paint, cleaning). Our case is the bigger, brief-based project work, plus consultants.
- **Our brand disappears.** Snoonu runs services under its own names (S Laundry, My House). A "Snoonu Projects, powered by Mimaary" offer would bring volume but less visibility. Decide what you accept before any meeting.
- **Privacy.** Owner details stay with Mimaary. Snoonu gets counts, not names, unless an owner agrees (Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016).
- **Money and pricing.** No fee, commission or revenue-share talk while pricing is undecided. Project money never passes through Mimaary or a Mimaary-branded checkout.
- **Lock-in.** No exclusivity and no rights to our data in a pilot agreement.
- **Their priorities change.** Jahez is under pressure, and a pilot can stop without notice. Treat Snoonu as one channel among several, never the plan.

## Next steps (Jassim decides each one)

1. **Look at House Services in the app (10 minutes).** Note the trades offered, fixed price or quote, any "custom request" option, and who the providers are. Take screenshots. This shows whether "bigger jobs" is a real gap.
2. **Decide on the Startup Factory conditions:** full-time at Snoonu HQ, a convertible loan from a corporate, a plan for a technical co-founder.
3. **Keep the first projects first.** A pitch with 3 to 5 real requests and offers returned is far stronger than one with none.
4. **Then make one approach.** If the conditions suit you, apply to the Early Stage track at startups.snoonu.com and propose the referral pilot in the application. If not, email business@snoonu.com, or better use a warm introduction, asking for a 20-minute call about the pilot. QDB led Snoonu's first two rounds and knows the team.
5. **Stop rule.** No reply within three weeks after one follow-up, or they only take fixed-price on-demand services: park it and revisit at growth gate 2 (10 introductions).

**Counting referrals.** A Snoonu link can carry `?utm_source=snoonu&utm_medium=referral`, but the site stores it only when the visitor accepts marketing cookies, so counts would be incomplete. Reliable counting needs a "How did you hear about us?" choice on the request form. That changes the submission payload and needs Jassim's approval; until then, ask on the intake call and log it.
