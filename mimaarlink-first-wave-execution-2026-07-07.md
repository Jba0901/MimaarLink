## 1. Chief Operator Decision

This week MimaarLink should not act like a broad services marketplace.

Primary wedge:

> Help Qatar project owners get comparable contractor/consultant responses for mid-ticket projects where bad scoping creates real pain.

Focus categories:

1. Villa renovation, extensions, bathrooms, kitchens, waterproofing.
2. Small commercial fit-out and renovation: offices, shops, clinics, cafes.
3. Consultant-led scope review, BOQ, supervision, and bid comparison.

Secondary categories:

- MEP/HVAC.
- Electrical/plumbing.
- Civil maintenance.
- Joinery/flooring/painting/aluminum as supporting trades.

Do not focus this week on:

- Micro handyman jobs.
- Emergency repairs.
- Public tender replacement.
- New villa build projects that directly compete with official/structured housing-loan channels.
- Software expansion before proving demand and paid provider behavior.

## 5. Provider Qualification Score

Use a 100-point score.

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Service fit | 20 |
| Evidence of Qatar work | 20 |
| Responsiveness | 15 |
| Quality process | 15 |
| Capacity | 10 |
| Commercial discipline | 10 |
| Trust signals | 10 |

Grades:

- A: 80-100. Priority shortlist.
- B: 65-79. Usable backup.
- C: 50-64. Keep but do not actively match yet.
- Reject: below 50 or serious red flag.

Baseline gate before scoring:

- Company name.
- Qatar phone/contact person.
- CR number or CR copy.
- Service category.
- Recent Qatar work evidence.
- For consultants: registration/classification/discipline details where relevant.

Hi, this is Jassim from MimaarLink.

Record the answer as:

## 7. Provider Call Questions

Keep first call under 8 minutes.

1. What is your exact company name and main service category?
2. Which work do you actually want more of in Qatar right now?
3. Which areas do you cover?
4. What project size is ideal for you?
5. What is too small or too large for you?
6. How quickly can you visit a site after a serious request?
7. How fast can you submit an itemized quote after site visit?
8. Do you have recent Qatar project photos, BOQs, completion letters, or references?
9. Do you use your own team, subcontractors, or both?
10. Who prepares quotations and who is authorized to approve pricing?
11. What warranty or defects period do you usually offer?
12. For consultants: which disciplines are you registered/classified for, and do you handle approvals?
13. If MimaarLink sends a matching request, who should receive it on WhatsApp?

## 9. Manual Matching SOP

### Project Intake

1. Open project within 15 minutes where possible.
2. Confirm project owner is reachable.
3. Confirm category, location, scope, photos/drawings, timeline, budget band.
4. Ask permission to share anonymized scope with providers.
5. Set document status:
   - Bid Ready
   - Quote Ready
   - Scoping Needed
   - Hold Risk
6. Build anonymized provider brief.
7. Move to matching only when owner and scope are real enough.

### Provider Matching

Use four filters:

- Category.
- Location.
- Project size.
- Document status.

Shortlist 5-8 possible providers maximum.

Provider-project statuses:

- Candidate.
- Teaser Sent.
- Interested.
- Bid Requested.
- Bid Received.
- Clarification Needed.
- Selected For Intro.
- Fee Due.
- Fee Paid.
- Introduced.
- Not Selected.
- No Response.

### Bid Collection

Required quote fields:

- Price.
- Scope included.
- Exclusions.
- Timeline.
- Payment terms.
- Materials/labor assumptions.
- Site visit requirement.
- Quote validity.

Only one clarification round. Maximum two follow-ups.

### Bid Comparison

Comparison must show:

- Provider label.
- Price.
- Timeline.
- Inclusions.
- Exclusions.
- Payment terms.
- Site visit need.
- Response quality.
- Risk notes.

Owner wording:

```text
بناءً على المعلومات المقدمة، هذه الخيارات تبدو الأقرب لنطاق مشروعك. الاختيار النهائي والاتفاق النهائي يكون بينك وبين مزود الخدمة مباشرة.
```

## 10. QAR 750 Fee Timing

Collect QAR 750 from the provider:

> After the owner selects the provider for direct introduction and before releasing owner contact, exact location, or arranging the direct meeting.

Fee unlocks:

- Formal introduction.
- Full contact handoff.
- Direct discussion continuation.

Fee does not guarantee:

- Winning the project.
- Contract signing.
- Owner payment.
- Project outcome.

Manual process:

1. Send payment request/invoice by WhatsApp/email with project ID.
2. Provider sends proof of payment.
3. Admin verifies receipt manually.
4. Mark `Fee Paid`.
5. Introduce owner and provider.

## 11. Handling-Time Cap

Target: below 2 hours per project.

Time budget:

- Screen: 10 min.
- Owner intake: 15 min.
- Brief: 15 min.
- Shortlist/outreach: 25 min.
- Bid chasing: 20 min.
- Comparison: 25 min.
- Fee/admin: 10 min.
- Notes: 10 min.

Hard rules:

- Max 8 providers per project.
- Max 2 provider follow-ups.
- One clarification round.
- Pause vague projects early.
- No free consulting before project is qualified.
- No direct intro before fee.

## 12. Kill Criteria

Kill or pause a project if:

- Owner unreachable after 3 attempts / 48 hours.
- Scope stays vague after one info request.
- Budget is clearly misaligned and owner will not adjust scope.
- Fewer than 2 relevant providers respond after one shortlist expansion.
- Provider fee unpaid after 24 hours.
- Either side asks MimaarLink to hold funds, guarantee outcomes, or manage final agreement.

Kill or pause a category if:

- After 30 targeted providers, fewer than 3 pass qualification.
- WhatsApp response below 15% after 50 messages.
- Provider response consistently below 20%.
- Selected providers refuse QAR 750.
- Admin handling exceeds 2 hours without a paid path.

Kill or pause paid-lead model if:

- Fewer than 20% of qualified providers answer yes or depends on project size to QAR 750.
- Providers see MimaarLink as spam lead-selling.
- Project-owner requests are not qualified enough to justify charging providers.

## 14. Evidence That Changes Direction

Change category focus if:

- Project owners submit different project types repeatedly.
- Providers in a category respond poorly after serious sourcing.
- Consultant-led scoping gets better owner/provider interest than contractor-only matching.

Change pricing if:

- Qualified providers reject QAR 750 despite strong opportunity quality.
- Providers accept higher fees for larger opportunities.
- Owners are willing to pay for consultant scoping before contractor bids.

Build more software only if:

- Manual handling repeatedly exceeds 2 hours for the same reason.
- Admin workflow bottleneck is repeated across at least 5 projects.
- Paid demand appears before heavy automation.
