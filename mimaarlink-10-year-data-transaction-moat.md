# MimaarLink 10-Year Data and Transaction Moat

Date: `2026-07-11`

## Chief Operator Decision

MimaarLink should aim to become Qatar's trusted private-project procurement intelligence layer, then expand through country-specific GCC cells. It should not try to become a broad contractor directory, a government-tender substitute, an escrow company, or a Procore clone.

The moat is a closed outcome loop:

`better scoped projects -> better provider invitations -> comparable bids -> better selections -> measured outcomes -> better future matching`

Profiles, listings, and generic reviews are copyable. Consented transaction history, normalized bid data, verified capability evidence, response behavior, and outcome records are harder to reproduce because they accumulate only by operating real projects.

## Immediate Priority Is Unchanged

Before the first QAR 750 fee:

- Do not build new moat software.
- Do not buy data or paid advertising.
- Do not process or hold customer money.
- Do not expand beyond villa renovation, commercial fit-out, and consultant-led scope/bid comparison.
- Capture the minimum structured fields manually while validating the selected-introduction model.

Long-term work is documentation and disciplined data capture only. The first collected fee remains the gating event.

## The Five Compounding Assets

### 1. Trust and Compliance Ledger

For each provider, record CR/licence evidence, permitted categories, document expiry, signatory confirmation when required, consultant classification where applicable, capacity, project-size fit, and evidence-check dates.

Public identity data is an input, not the moat. MOCI already provides business data and QR-based CR/licence verification. MimaarLink's private value is whether a provider responds, bids appropriately, honors its scope, and performs on the type and size of work claimed.

### 2. Scope and Cost Taxonomy

Normalize real private-project scopes into reusable categories, work packages, inclusions, exclusions, location zones, budget bands, timelines, warranties, and consultant requirements.

Over time, this becomes a Qatar-specific scope-quality and price-range index. It should be reported only in sufficiently aggregated, anonymized form.

### 3. Provider Capability and Behavior Graph

Measure which providers are suitable for which work, not who pays most:

- Invite response time and bid/no-bid reason.
- Category, location, project-size, and urgency fit.
- Quote completeness and exclusion quality.
- Shortlist and selection rates.
- Fee acceptance and cleared payment.
- Award, completion, delay, change-order, warranty, and dispute outcomes where confirmed.

Do not hide paid ranking inside matching. Commercial payments may buy access to a qualified opportunity, never undisclosed ranking preference.

### 4. Referral and Demand Graph

Record which property managers, brokers, showrooms, consultants, owners, and repeat customers generate qualified projects. Measure project quality, conversion, repeat rate, and category fit by source.

This creates a defensible distribution network without scraping individual users or purchasing questionable contact lists.

### 5. Transaction Workflow

Own the workflow around the transaction before trying to own the money:

- Structured intake.
- Scope completeness check.
- Provider invitation and response tracking.
- Normalized bid comparison.
- Owner selection record.
- Provider fee acknowledgment and invoice.
- Direct handoff after cleared payment.
- Outcome and issue follow-up.

Later payments should be integrated through a licensed bank or payment-service provider unless competent advice confirms a different lawful structure. MimaarLink should not hold client or project funds merely to appear more complete.

## Minimum Data To Capture Now

No new software is required. Add these fields to the manual operating record for every real project.

### Project

- Internal project ID; source/referrer; owner segment.
- Qatar zone; category/subcategory; project-size and budget band.
- Scope completeness; photos/drawings present; consultant needed.
- Start window; decision authority; anonymized sharing consent.

### Invite and Bid

- Provider ID and evidence status at invite time.
- Invite timestamp; response timestamp; bid/no-bid and reason.
- Price; duration; validity; inclusions; exclusions; warranty; attachments.
- Admin time spent and intervention required.

### Outcome

- Shortlisted and selected provider IDs.
- QAR 750 acceptance, invoice, cleared-payment, and handoff timestamps.
- Awarded/not awarded; final value when confirmed.
- Completion, delay, change-order, warranty, dispute, and satisfaction signals when confirmed.
- Evidence source and confidence. Never convert an allegation into a performance fact.

Keep analytics IDs separate from unnecessary owner personal data.

## Gated Roadmap

### Gate 0: First Paid Introduction

Exit evidence:

- One real scoped project.
- Owner selects a provider.
- Provider accepts and pays QAR 750 before handoff.
- Finance verifies cleared funds.

Build allowed: none beyond manual logs and essential bug fixes.

### Gate 1: 10 Paid Introductions

Build only the internal data foundation:

- Stable taxonomy and evidence definitions.
- Provider/project scoring rules with human review.
- Bid normalization template.
- Consent, retention, correction, and access procedures.

### Gate 2: 50 Paid Introductions in Two Repeatable Categories

Build operational software where repetition is proven:

- Provider dashboard and document-expiry reminders.
- Project invitations and structured bid submission.
- Internal match recommendations with explainable reasons.
- Owner comparison view and outcome follow-up.

### Gate 3: 100 Paid Introductions and Repeat Demand

Test recurring revenue:

- Provider subscription only where lead volume repeats.
- Owner/consultant workflow fee for deeper scope and bid normalization.
- Aggregate category benchmarks with privacy thresholds.
- Licensed payment-link integration; no MimaarLink custody of project funds.

### Gate 4: Qatar Density

Required before GCC expansion:

- At least 100 paid introductions with reliable category-level data.
- Two categories with repeat owner demand and repeat provider participation.
- Manual handling below one hour for routine projects.
- Documented privacy, security, correction, retention, and dispute processes.
- No unresolved CR/activity, invoice, or payment-regulatory blocker.

### Gate 5: One GCC Country Cell

Enter one city and category through a local operating partner. Reuse the common project/bid/outcome schema while keeping identity verification, contracting, privacy, payments, licences, and claims country-specific.

Do not launch a single undifferentiated GCC marketplace. Density in one local graph is more valuable than shallow listings across six countries.

## Commercial Layers

Introduce these only in sequence:

1. Selected-introduction fee: QAR 750 minimum validation offer.
2. Higher qualified-opportunity fees based on evidenced project value and service level.
3. Provider subscription after recurring opportunity volume exists.
4. Owner or consultant workflow fee for structured scope and bid normalization.
5. Aggregate benchmark products for professional users, with minimum sample and privacy rules.
6. Licensed payment and financing partnerships, without unlicensed custody or promises.

The core economic metric is gross profit per successfully selected and paid introduction, not registered users or bid volume.

## Lawful Data Constitution

- Collect only data needed for matching, trust, transaction, support, or lawful analytics.
- State why data is collected and who receives it.
- Obtain consent or another confirmed lawful basis before sharing personal/project information.
- Allow correction and handle access/deletion requests through a documented process.
- Minimize retention of owner identity documents, precise addresses, signatures, and private drawings.
- Keep private documents access-controlled and separate from aggregate analytics.
- Do not scrape restricted sources, republish government data outside its licence, or claim government verification/partnership without authorization.
- Use aggregated benchmarks only when re-identification risk is acceptably low.
- Record provenance, check date, confidence, and correction history for important facts.

Qatar's PDPPL guidance treats a business deciding why and how personal data is processed as a controller and requires appropriate controller/processor arrangements. This needs professional implementation review as MimaarLink's data use expands.

## Strategic Position Relative To Public Systems

- MOCI/Qatar Business Map: authoritative public identity and licence-check input; MimaarLink adds private operating outcomes.
- Monaqasat: unified state-procurement and supplier-classification system; MimaarLink remains focused on private mid-ticket owner projects.
- Aqarat: signals Qatar's direction toward real-estate data and analytics; MimaarLink should complement, not impersonate, national infrastructure.
- Qatar Open Data: useful for aggregate market context. The published building-permit dataset explicitly excludes maintenance permits, which suggests an analytical blind spot but does not prove private-market exclusivity.
- QCB-regulated payment systems: future transaction convenience should come from licensed rails and partners, not unauthorized fund custody.

## Moat KPIs

Track these from the first transaction:

- Qualified-project rate by source and category.
- Median time from intake to complete scope.
- Provider invite response and valid-bid rates.
- Bid completeness and comparison readiness.
- Owner shortlist and selection rates.
- QAR 750 acceptance and collection rates.
- Time spent per project and gross profit per paid introduction.
- Repeat owner/referrer and provider participation.
- Completion, change-order, warranty, and dispute rates with evidence confidence.
- Data-correction, deletion, access, security, and document-expiry events.

## Kill Conditions

- If fewer than 3 interested replies follow 30 approved qualified approaches, stop that message/channel.
- If providers consistently reject QAR 750 despite real owner selection, reassess opportunity quality and payment timing before lowering price.
- If 10 paid introductions do not improve matching speed or selection quality, the proposed data loop is not yet a moat.
- If MimaarLink must perform unpaid consulting or project management to make each introduction work, narrow the offer or charge for that service within lawful CR coverage.
- If a new revenue layer requires activities, permissions, data use, or payment handling not covered by current approvals, stop at the boundary and obtain competent review before launch.
